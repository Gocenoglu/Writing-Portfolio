BOOK CHAPTERS (TURKISH)
"Kurumsal Gönüllülük ve Özel Sektörde Sosyal Sorumluluk Projeleri" ("Corporate Volunteering and Social Responsibility Projects in the Private Sector") — Chapter 15, Gönüllülük Kitabı (The Volunteering Book), Istanbul University Press, 2022. DOI: 10.26650/B/SS49.2022.001.15
A peer-reviewed academic book chapter examining corporate volunteering frameworks, engagement models, and the value created for both companies and nonprofit partners, including a case study of IBM's Corporate Service Corps program — a global volunteer initiative the author personally led in Turkey (2013–2017).
Sole author — Dr. Ceyhun Gocenoglu.
Published in Turkish for an academic readership. Directly on-subject for this assignment: peer-reviewed scholarship specifically on corporate volunteering, the same field as UNICEF's Global Volunteer Initiative under which Green Rising operates. An English-language summary of this chapter can be provided on request.
